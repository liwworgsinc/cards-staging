Questions

Restaurant digital business card FAQ

Can I link my menu?

Yes. Link to the menu page, PDF or public menu destination you currently use.

Can I connect online ordering or reservations?

Yes. Add supported links to the ordering or reservation provider your business already uses.

Can diners scan the card from a table sign?

Yes. Your LIW Card QR code can be used on supported printed material so guests can scan it with their phone.

Can I change menu or ordering links later?

Yes. Update the links in your card while keeping the same public card address.
