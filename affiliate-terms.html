LIW Cards Affiliate Program Agreement

This Agreement governs affiliate participation with LIW Cards operated by LIW WORGS INC. It supplements the main Terms of Service.

Current commission policy: 25% on eligible Plus/Pro first purchases and 15% on eligible Agency billing during an affiliate’s first 12 months. After that, standard rates are 10% for new eligible Plus/Pro first purchases and 5% for eligible Agency billing. Plus/Pro renewals do not generate lifetime affiliate commission.

Important agreement information. Tap any section to read it. The complete agreement remains available here at all times.

1. Enrollment and participation

Affiliate access is included with eligible LIW Cards accounts, but participation may be optional where the account interface provides an opt-in or opt-out control. A unique referral code or link is provided when affiliate earning is active. Link access does not guarantee commission payment. Before payout, the account holder must accept this Agreement, provide required tax and payout information, and remain in good standing. LIW may pause or terminate participation for fraud risk, brand-safety concerns, policy violations, or unlawful conduct.

2. Referral attribution

The standard anonymous tracking window is 30 days. During that window, the first valid affiliate referral recorded for the visitor is preserved rather than replaced by a later referral link. When that referral becomes linked to a signed-in LIW Cards account, the linked affiliate attribution is retained for that account so later URL changes or a different referral link do not replace it. Browser settings, deleted storage before account linkage, device changes before sign-in, customer actions, and technical limitations may prevent anonymous tracking. Self-referrals are not eligible.

3. Commission schedule and first-year boost

Unless a written campaign states otherwise, an affiliate receives boosted rates for the first 12 months after that affiliate account is created: an eligible referred customer’s first paid Plus or Pro card-plan purchase earns 25% of net qualifying revenue, and eligible Agency Starter or Agency Pro billing earns 15%. After the affiliate’s 12-month anniversary, standard rates are 10% for a new eligible referred customer’s first paid Plus or Pro card-plan purchase and 5% for eligible Agency billing. Plus/Pro commission applies only to the referred customer’s first eligible paid card-plan purchase; recurring Plus/Pro card-plan renewals do not generate lifetime affiliate commission. Agency Starter or Agency Pro billing can generate commission only during the first 12 months measured from that referred Agency customer’s first eligible Agency commission, with no more than 12 eligible commission-bearing payments during that window. If the affiliate’s personal boost expires during the referred Agency customer’s eligible window, later eligible Agency payments use the 5% standard Agency rate. Free and Lite signups or purchases do not earn commission. Taxes, credits, discounts, refunds, chargebacks, disputed payments, fraudulent transactions, and processing reversals do not earn commission.

4. Pending period and payouts

Commissions remain pending for at least 30 days and may remain pending longer during investigation. Approved balances are generally paid monthly after reaching the $25 minimum. LIW primarily sends affiliate payouts through Zelle. Venmo or Cash App may be selected as an alternative payout method where available. LIW may offset later refunds or chargebacks against current or future commissions.

5. Tax and payout information

Affiliates must provide accurate taxpayer and payout information before payment. Payout information must belong to the affiliate. LIW is not responsible for delayed, rejected, or misdirected payments caused by incorrect information supplied by the affiliate. U.S. persons will generally be asked for Form W-9 information. Non-U.S. individuals may be asked for Form W-8BEN, while non-U.S. entities may need another Form W-8. LIW may withhold payment until documentation is complete and may issue required information returns. Affiliates are responsible for their own taxes.

6. Required disclosures

Affiliates must clearly and conspicuously disclose that they may earn a commission. The disclosure must appear close to the recommendation or link and must be understandable before the customer acts.

7. Prohibited conduct

No self-referrals, fake accounts, cookie stuffing, forced redirects, spam, purchased or misleading reviews, impersonation, unauthorized trademark bidding, unauthorized coupons, false scarcity, deceptive income claims, or unlawful email or text marketing. Affiliates may not imply employment, partnership, or authority to bind LIW.

8. Brand use

LIW grants a limited, revocable, nonexclusive right to use approved program materials solely to promote LIW Cards. Affiliates may not alter logos in a misleading way, register confusing domains, or create pages that appear to be owned by LIW.

9. Independent contractor

Affiliates are independent contractors, not employees, agents, franchisees, or joint venturers. No benefits, guaranteed earnings, or reimbursement are provided.

10. Monitoring and records

LIW may request examples of promotions and records supporting compliance. Affiliates must promptly correct or remove noncompliant content.

11. Suspension and termination

Either party may end participation. LIW may suspend tracking or withhold unpaid commissions reasonably connected to fraud, breach, invalid traffic, or prohibited conduct. Valid approved commissions earned before ordinary termination remain subject to the pending period, offsets, and minimum payout.

12. Changes

LIW may update commission rates, tracking, eligibility, and program rules prospectively. Material updates will be posted or communicated through the affiliate account. Changes do not retroactively reduce commissions already validly earned and recorded before an effective policy change unless an adjustment is required for a refund, chargeback, fraud, or another ineligible transaction.

13. Main terms

The liability, warranty, dispute, governing-law, and privacy provisions in the LIW Cards Terms of Service also apply. Where this Agreement conflicts on affiliate matters, this Agreement controls.
