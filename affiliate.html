Transparent commission structure

Your first 12 months as an affiliate are boosted: an eligible referred customer’s first Plus or Pro paid purchase pays 25%, and eligible Agency Starter and Agency Pro billing pays 15%. After your affiliate boost ends, new eligible Plus/Pro first purchases use 10% and eligible Agency billing uses 5%. Plus/Pro renewals do not pay lifetime affiliate commission. Free and Lite are not commission-paying plans.

Affiliate program questions

Affiliate program FAQs

Is it free to become an affiliate?

Yes. Affiliate access is included automatically with every LIW Cards account. There is no joining fee or separate application.

Do affiliates create or manage customer cards?

No. Affiliates refer customers using their tracked link. Your Free, Lite, Plus, or Pro card plan controls what you can build for yourself.

Does the Lite plan pay affiliate commission?

Not currently. Lite gives referrals a low-cost step between Free and Plus, but the current commission-paying individual plans are Plus and Pro. Eligible Agency Starter and Agency Pro billing can also generate commission.

How does the first-year commission boost work?

Your affiliate account receives boosted rates for its first 12 months: 25% on an eligible referred customer’s first paid Plus/Pro purchase and 15% on eligible Agency billing. After your 12-month affiliate anniversary, new eligible Plus/Pro first purchases use 10% and eligible Agency billing uses 5%. Plus/Pro renewals do not pay lifetime affiliate commission.

Can I earn on Agency plans?

Yes. Eligible Agency Starter and Agency Pro billing can generate commission only during the first 12 months from that referred Agency customer’s first eligible Agency commission, with no more than 12 eligible commission-bearing payments. If your personal first-year boost ends during that window, later eligible Agency payments use the 5% standard rate.

When are commissions paid?

Approved commissions are generally paid monthly after the 30-day review hold and once the payable balance reaches $25. Agreement, tax, identity, and payout requirements must also be complete.

How will I receive my affiliate payout?

Zelle is the recommended payout method. Affiliates may choose Venmo or Cash App as an alternative.

Can I use my own link to purchase?

No. Self-referrals and attempts to generate commission from your own purchases are prohibited.

What disclosure should I use?

A clear statement such as “I may earn a commission if you purchase through my link” should appear close to the recommendation or referral link.
