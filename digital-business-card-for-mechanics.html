Mechanic digital business card FAQ

Can I use LIW Cards if I am a mobile mechanic?

Yes. Your card can focus on contact actions, service-area information and the links customers need without requiring a storefront.

Can I list the services I provide?

You can link to service information, and eligible plans can support richer service content directly on the card.

Can I change my shop address later?

Yes. Update your card details while keeping the same public card link.

Can I put the QR code on printed material?

Yes. The QR can be used on supported print material so customers can scan and save your digital card.
