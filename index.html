Questions

Before you get started

Can I build a card before paying?

Yes. Free lets you create and publish one public card with core templates, QR, contact saving, social links, and LIW WORGS INC branding.

Do customers need an app?

No. Your card opens in any modern mobile browser. Customers can save your contact details without installing anything.

Can I change my information later?

Yes. Update your card anytime. Your existing link and QR code continue to work.

Can I create cards for staff or locations?

Yes. Pro includes up to 10 published cards. Agency plans are available when you need dedicated client-card capacity and agency workflows.
