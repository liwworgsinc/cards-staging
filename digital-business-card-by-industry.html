Why industry pages matter

People often search for a solution in the context of their own business. Someone searching for a digital business card for a barber is usually thinking about booking and client retention, while a realtor may care more about open-house follow-up and referrals.

Industry-specific pages let LIW Cards answer those needs directly, then send the visitor into the same core product with a more relevant message.

  • Clearer search intent than a generic feature page
  • Stronger internal links between related LIW Cards solutions
  • More useful examples for each type of business
  • A direct build-free CTA tied to the visitor's industry
  • Reusable structure for future industries as the catalog expands
