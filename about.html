Why LIW Cards exists

Business has changed. The business card should too.

People connect through phones, social platforms, QR codes, messages, email, and websites every day. Yet the traditional business card still asks someone to hold onto a small piece of paper and remember what to do with it later.

LIW Cards takes a more useful approach. Your card lives online, stays current, and can bring your contact information, business links, services, products, content, booking options, payments, and other tools into one shareable experience.

The goal is simple: reduce friction between meeting someone and doing business with them.

The old wayHand over information and hope it gets used.

Paper can be misplaced, details become outdated, and the next step is not always obvious.

The LIW approachShare one living business connection.

Update your information without reprinting and keep the same card working wherever you share it.

Give people a clearer path from discovering you to contacting, booking, buying, or referring your business.
