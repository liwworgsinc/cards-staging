Install your signature

Copy the finished signature above, then paste it into your email provider.

Gmail

Open Gmail Settings → See all settings → General → Signature. Create or edit a signature, paste the copied LIW signature, then save changes.

Outlook

Open Outlook Settings → Accounts → Signatures. Create or edit a signature, paste the copied LIW signature, choose your default account, then save.

Apple Mail

On Mac, open Mail → Settings → Signatures. Add a signature, replace the default text with the copied LIW signature, then assign it to your account.
