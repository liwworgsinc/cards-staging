Questions

Nail artist digital business card FAQ

Can I add my booking link?

Yes. Link your LIW Card to the public booking or scheduling destination you already use.

Can clients see my nail work?

You can link to your social portfolio or website, and eligible plans can support richer content sections.

Can I put the QR code at my nail station?

Yes. You can display the QR code digitally or on supported printed material so clients can scan your card in person.

Can I update my card when prices or links change?

Yes. Edit the card while keeping the same public link you have already shared.
