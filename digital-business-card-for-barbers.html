Where barbers can use a digital business card

Barbering is referral-driven. A client who likes your work is often the person who introduces the next client. Your card should be easy to pull up, easy to share and clear about how to book you.

On the mirror or station

Use a printed QR code or display the code from your device so a client can save your card, follow your social page or book the next appointment while they are already in the shop.

In your social bio

Use the shareable link as a central destination for contact, booking and business information instead of forcing visitors to hunt through posts and highlights.

When someone asks for your barber

Your clients can send your digital card link directly to a friend. That referral gets your current information rather than an old screenshot or typed phone number.

Questions

Barber digital business card FAQ

Can I put my booking link on my card?

Yes. You can link to a public booking or scheduling page you use so clients can move from your card to an appointment destination.

Can I put the QR code at my barber station?

Yes. Your QR code can be displayed digitally or used on supported printed material so clients can scan your card in the shop.

Can I show my haircut portfolio?

You can connect visitors to your social and website portfolio links. Eligible plans can also support richer content sections.

What happens if I change shops?

Update your location and business details in LIW Cards while keeping the same public card link you have already shared.
