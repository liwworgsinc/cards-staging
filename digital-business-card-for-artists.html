How artists and musicians can use a digital business card

An artist card can work like a compact professional hub. The goal is not to replace every platform you use. It is to give fans and business contacts a clean starting point that connects those platforms together.

At shows and events

Display your QR code on a phone, table card, flyer or other supported material. Someone who just heard your set can scan while the moment is still fresh and choose where to listen, follow or contact you.

For promoters and collaborators

Send one card link containing your professional identity, contact details and key media destinations. That is easier to manage than rebuilding a message every time someone asks for your information.

During a release campaign

Change your featured links as the campaign moves from pre-save to release day to video promotion. Your main card URL can stay the same while the destinations behind it evolve.

  • Video and social profiles

Artist digital business card FAQ

Can I put my music links on the card?

Yes. You can add supported links to the public music destinations and platforms you use.

Can I use the card for booking inquiries?

Yes. Add the professional email, phone, website or other supported contact destination you want promoters and clients to use.

Can I change the featured release later?

Yes. Update your card as your campaign changes while keeping the same public card link.

Can fans scan a QR code at a show?

Yes. Your LIW Card QR code can be shown on screen or used on supported print material so people can open the card quickly.
