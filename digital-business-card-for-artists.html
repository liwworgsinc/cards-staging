One creator hub instead of a pile of disconnected links

Showtime is designed to keep the important parts of a performer or creator identity in one audience-friendly place. It does not replace YouTube, Spotify, a podcast host, ticketing service or social network. It gives people one starting point that connects those destinations together.

For musicians, DJs and producers

Lead with music, featured releases, videos, shows, fan actions, social and booking. Release artwork can be uploaded while the actual listen destination stays on the platform you choose.

For comedians and actors

Put videos, upcoming appearances, gallery/media, social and Book Me up front. Your card can be performance-first without pretending you are a recording artist.

For podcasters and creators

Feature your podcast or show, connect video and media, keep social destinations close, and give brands, collaborators or clients a direct Book Me path.

  • Choose a performer / creator type and recommended setup
  • Turn relevant home actions on or off
  • Music releases and artwork when your work includes music
  • Shows, dates and ticket links for live appearances
  • Podcast link, videos, gallery, press and other media
  • Book Me across every Showtime plan level
  • Products / merch on Plus and Pro
Questions

Showtime FAQ

Is Showtime only for musicians?

No. Showtime supports rappers, singers, musicians, bands, DJs, producers, comedians, podcasters, actors/performers, creators/influencers and other artists or creators. You choose the setup that fits what you do.

Is Showtime only on a paid plan?

No. The Showtime experience is available on Free, Lite, Plus and Pro. Plan limits increase as you move up.

Do the music limits matter if I am a podcaster or comedian?

Only if you use the Music Releases module. A podcaster can focus on Podcast, Media, Social and Book Me; a comedian can focus on Shows, Videos, Gallery and Book Me.

Does Free include Book Me?

Yes. Book Me is available across Free, Lite, Plus and Pro so a new performer or creator can receive professional inquiries without upgrading first.

Do I upload my whole gallery to LIW?

No. Gallery and media items use URLs for video, photos, press and other destinations. Release artwork can be uploaded separately when you use Music Releases.

When can I add products or merch?

Products begin on Plus with up to 4 and increase to up to 12 on Pro.
