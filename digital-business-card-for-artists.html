How artists and musicians can use a digital business card

An artist card can work like a compact professional hub. The goal is not to replace every platform you use. It is to give fans and business contacts a clean starting point that connects those platforms together.

At shows and events

Display your QR code on a phone, table card, flyer or other supported material. Someone who just heard your set can scan while the moment is still fresh and choose where to listen, follow or contact you.

For promoters and collaborators

Send one card link containing your professional identity, booking path, key media destinations and upcoming dates. That is easier to manage than rebuilding a message every time someone asks for your information.

During a release campaign

Change your featured links as the campaign changes while keeping the same main card URL. Your plan controls how many releases and shows you can keep active at once.

  • Music streaming and release links
  • Upcoming shows and ticket links

Questions

Artist digital business card FAQ

Is the Showtime Artist Card only on a paid plan?

No. The Showtime Artist Card experience is available on Free, Lite, Plus and Pro. Limits increase as you move up the plans.

Can I put my music links on the card?

Yes. Add releases and supported links to the public music destinations and platforms you use.

Does Free include Book Me?

Yes. Book Me is available across Free, Lite, Plus and Pro so even a new artist can receive professional booking inquiries.

Do I upload my whole gallery to LIW?

Gallery and media items use URLs for video, photos, press and other destinations. Release artwork can be uploaded separately.

When can I add merch?

Products / merch begin on Plus with up to 4 products and increase to up to 12 on Pro.

Can fans scan a QR code at a show?

Yes. Your LIW Card QR code can be shown on screen or used on supported print material so people can open the card quickly.
