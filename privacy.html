Privacy Policy

How LIW Digital Cards collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects account information, public card content, visitor leads, analytics, and purchase information.

Effective: July 24, 2026Last updated: July 24, 2026

1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LIW WORGS INC, which operates LIW Digital Cards (“LIW,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the LIW Digital Cards website, account system, card builder, dashboard, published cards, analytics, checkout, products, and support services.

This Policy applies to account holders, card visitors, prospective customers, and people who contact us. It does not govern websites or services controlled by third parties.

Important: Information placed on a published digital card is intentionally public. Do not publish anything you want to keep private.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email address, password credentials managed by our authentication provider, and account preferences.
  • Card content: display name, title, company, biography, phone, email, website, address, photo, branding choices, social links, services, products, prices, booking links, payment links, and custom-domain requests.
  • Lead and inquiry information: names, contact details, messages, and other information visitors submit through an enabled card form.
  • Purchase and support information: plan, add-on, order, billing status, shipping details for physical products, correspondence, revision requests, and support messages.

Information collected automatically

When the Service is used, we and our providers may collect device and browser type, IP address, date and time, referring page, requested page, card views, contact saves, button clicks, link interactions, authentication events, error logs, and similar security or performance information.

Information from service providers

We may receive transaction status, customer identifiers, subscription details, fraud signals, delivery status, authentication status, and technical logs from providers that help operate the Service.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and secure accounts;
  • save, publish, display, and update digital cards;
  • provide QR codes, contact files, analytics, leads, domains, subscriptions, products, and support;
  • process transactions, send receipts, prevent duplicate subscriptions, and manage cancellations;
  • deliver physical products and perform requested design or setup work;
  • detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical problems;
  • communicate service notices, support replies, billing information, and material legal updates;
  • understand feature usage and improve usability, reliability, and performance;
  • comply with law, enforce agreements, and protect rights and safety.

We do not use an account holder’s signup name as the automatic display name for a new card. Card owners choose the information displayed on each card.

4. Published cards and visitor leads

Published card information

A card marked “published” can be viewed by anyone with its link, QR code, connected domain, or access through sharing or indexing. Public card information may be copied, saved, photographed, indexed, or redistributed by others. Unpublishing or deleting a card stops future access through LIW Digital Cards but cannot retrieve copies already saved elsewhere.

Lead forms

When a visitor submits an inquiry through a card, the information is made available to the card owner and stored in LIW Digital Cards so the owner can respond. The card owner is independently responsible for how they use exported or copied lead information and for any notices or consent their business requires.

5. How information is disclosed

We may disclose information:

  • At your direction: when you publish a card, share a link, connect a domain, enable a form, or purchase a service.
  • To card owners: when a visitor submits a lead or inquiry through that owner’s card.
  • To service providers: companies supporting authentication, database hosting, file storage, website hosting, content delivery, payments, email, QR codes, domains, security, shipping, support, and analytics.
  • For legal and safety reasons: when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, enforce agreements, investigate abuse, or protect users, the public, LIW Digital Cards, or others.
  • During a business transaction: in connection with financing, merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate protections.

LIW Digital Cards does not sell personal information for money and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may disclose aggregated or de-identified information that does not reasonably identify an individual.

6. Payments

Stripe processes subscription and one-time payments. LIW Digital Cards receives information such as customer and subscription identifiers, product purchased, amount, currency, status, invoice information, and limited billing or shipping details. LIW Digital Cards does not receive or store the complete payment-card number or security code. Stripe’s handling of payment information is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

8. Cookies, sessions, and local storage

LIW Digital Cards and its providers may use cookies, browser local storage, session storage, and similar technologies to keep users signed in, protect accounts, remember workflow state, route users after login, prevent fraud, maintain preferences, and measure performance. Blocking essential storage may prevent login or other features from working.

LIW Digital Cards currently does not use these technologies for third-party behavioral advertising.

9. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Service, maintain an account, process orders, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud, satisfy tax or accounting requirements, and comply with law. Retention periods vary by data type.

  • Deleted cards and their directly connected links, products, services, leads, views, events, and domain requests are removed from the active database through the deletion process.
  • Account, billing, support, security, and legal records may be retained longer when required or reasonably necessary.
  • Residual copies may remain temporarily in encrypted backups or provider systems until normal deletion cycles complete.

10. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information, including authenticated access, row-level data controls, transport encryption provided by hosting platforms, restricted service credentials, and payment processing through Stripe. No online system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access will never occur.

Use a unique password, protect your email account, log out of shared devices, and notify us promptly about suspected unauthorized access. Do not send highly sensitive information through ordinary email or publish it on a card.

11. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent; or appeal a denied request. California residents may also have rights to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, limit certain uses of sensitive information, and receive equal service when exercising privacy rights.

LIW Digital Cards does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will honor legally required browser-based opt-out signals when applicable to our practices.

Account holders can edit or delete card information through the dashboard. To request account-level access, correction, deletion, or another privacy action, email liwworgsinc@gmail.com. We may verify identity before completing a request. Authorized agents may be required to provide proof of authority.

12. Children’s privacy

LIW Digital Cards is a business service intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

13. Processing in the United States

LIW Digital Cards is operated from New York, United States. Information may be processed and stored in the United States or other locations where our providers operate. Privacy protections may differ from those in your home jurisdiction.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service, providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the date at the top and may provide additional notice for material changes.
