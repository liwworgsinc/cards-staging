Billing questions

Clear terms before checkout.

How do the Plus and Pro trials work?

Eligible first-time customers receive 7 days free with a payment method on file. Plus then renews at $49/year and Pro at $99/year unless canceled before the trial ends. Each account receives one plan trial.

Does Free include the Email Signature Generator?

Free accounts can design and preview an email signature. Copy Signature, Copy HTML, and HTML download are included with Plus, Pro, and Agency plans.

Are Plus and Pro monthly subscriptions?

No. Plus is $49 per year and Pro is $99 per year, paid annually after any eligible trial.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Subscription cancellation stops the next renewal. Access continues through the paid term unless a refund is legally required or expressly approved under the refund policy.
