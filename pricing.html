Billing questions

Clear terms before checkout.

What is Showtime?

Showtime is LIW Cards’ audience-first experience for artists, performers and creators. It supports rappers, singers, musicians, bands, DJs, producers, comedians, podcasters, actors/performers, creators/influencers and other artists or creators.

Is Showtime only available on a paid plan?

No. Showtime is available on Free, Lite, Plus and Pro. Music release capacity is 1, 3, 10 and 25; show/date capacity is 1, 3, 10 and 25. Products begin on Plus.

Is Showtime only for musicians?

No. The music numbers apply only when you use the Music Releases module. A comedian can focus on Shows, Videos, Gallery and Book Me; a podcaster can focus on Podcast, Media, Social and Book Me.

Does Book Me work on Free Showtime?

Yes. Book Me is included on Free, Lite, Plus and Pro so a new performer or creator can receive professional inquiries without upgrading first.

Does Lite have a free trial?

No. Free is available indefinitely, so Lite begins immediately when activated. Choose $2.49/month or $24/year.

How much do I save with Lite yearly?

Twelve monthly Lite payments total $29.88. Lite yearly is $24, saving $5.88.

How do the Plus and Pro trials work?

The 7-day trial applies to eligible first-time annual Plus and Pro customers. Plus monthly is $5.99/month with no trial. Annual Plus renews at $49/year and Pro at $99/year unless canceled before the trial ends.

Does Lite include Premium Templates or Flow?

No. Lite uses Core Templates. It can use the Classic or Showtime card experience, while Flow remains a Pro feature.

Does Free or Lite include the Email Signature Generator?

Free and Lite accounts can design and preview an email signature. Copy Signature, Copy HTML, and HTML download are included with Plus, Pro, and Agency plans.

Which plans have monthly billing?

Lite offers $2.49/month or $24/year. Plus offers $5.99/month or $49/year. Pro is $99/year.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Subscription cancellation stops the next renewal. Access continues through the paid term unless a refund is legally required or expressly approved under the refund policy.
