Tax onboarding

Documents required before affiliate payout

LIW does not ask affiliates to place Social Security numbers or taxpayer identification numbers into an ordinary website form. Approved affiliates receive a secure tax-information request before payout.

United States persons

U.S. individuals and businesses are generally asked to complete IRS Form W-9 so LIW can collect the legal name, address and taxpayer identification information needed for reporting.

View official IRS Form W-9

Non-U.S. affiliates

A non-U.S. individual is commonly asked for Form W-8BEN. A foreign entity may require Form W-8BEN-E or another Form W-8 depending on its classification. LIW may request professional tax guidance where classification is unclear.

View official IRS Form W-8BEN

Information returns

LIW will track affiliate payments and issue any information return required under the rules applicable to the payment year. Tax thresholds and reporting rules can change, so the company should confirm them annually with its accountant or tax preparer.

Recommended operational process

  1. Approve the affiliate application.
  2. Send a secure W-9 or W-8 request through a tax-document or payout provider.
  3. Mark tax status as verified in the LIW admin system.
  4. Confirm the affiliate’s Zelle, Venmo, or Cash App information and do not release payouts until tax and payout details are complete.
  5. Reconcile annual payments and prepare required forms after year-end.

This page provides operational information, not personal tax advice.
