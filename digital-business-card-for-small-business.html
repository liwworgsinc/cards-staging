How small businesses can use a digital card

A strong small-business card should answer three questions quickly: who are you, what do you do, and what should the customer do next? That means your card should not be packed with random links. It should be organized around the actions that matter most to your business.

At networking events

Show the QR code on your phone and let the person open your card immediately. They can save your contact details while the conversation is still fresh.

On printed marketing

Add your QR code to flyers, table signs, business cards, packaging or other supported materials so print can lead people into a richer digital experience.

On social media and text

Use your shareable card link when someone asks for your information. Instead of sending multiple separate links, send one destination that keeps your core business details together.

  • Phone and text contact
  • Website and social profiles
  • Service and product information on eligible plans
  • Booking, payment or inquiry destinations where supported
  • Analytics and deeper business tools on eligible plans
