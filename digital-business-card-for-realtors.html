How real estate agents can use a digital card

A realtor's business card has to work in more places than an office. You may meet someone at a showing, community event, closing, networking mixer or just while talking about the neighborhood. A shareable digital card gives you one consistent professional identity across all of those moments.

At an open house

Place your QR code where visitors can scan it, or show it directly from your phone. Your card can connect them to your contact details and the listing destinations you choose to feature.

After a referral

When a past client introduces you by text, send one card link instead of a string of separate links. The new prospect gets your contact information, website and other important destinations in one place.

When a listing changes

Because your card is editable, you can update featured links and business information without changing the main card URL you have already shared.

  • Professional photo, title and brokerage information
  • Phone, text, email and contact saving
  • Listing, website and social links
  • Booking or inquiry destinations where supported
  • Lead and analytics tools on eligible plans
Questions

Realtor digital business card FAQ

Can I link to my real estate listings?

Yes. You can add supported website and external links that direct visitors to your listing page, brokerage profile, IDX site or another public destination you use.

Can I use the QR code at open houses?

Yes. Your LIW Card QR code can be displayed on a phone or used on supported print materials so visitors can scan your card.

Can I change my brokerage or phone number later?

Yes. Your card is editable, so you can update professional information without replacing the public card link.

Do prospects need an app?

No. Your public LIW Card opens in the browser, so the visitor does not need to install an LIW Cards app.
