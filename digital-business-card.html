What is a digital business card?

A digital business card is a mobile-friendly web page that presents your professional identity and contact information through a shareable link or QR code. Instead of handing someone a printed card and hoping they keep it, you can give them a page they can open immediately on the phone already in their hand.

For entrepreneurs and small businesses, the advantage is flexibility. Your card can connect people to your phone number, email address, website, social profiles and other important destinations while giving you room to present more of what your business does.

Digital business card vs. paper business card

Paper still has a place, especially when you want something physical to hand someone. The difference is that a digital card stays editable. When information changes, you update the card instead of throwing away a box of outdated prints.

Questions

Digital business card FAQ

Is there a free digital business card plan?

Yes. LIW Cards has a Free plan that lets you start with one published digital card and core sharing features. Paid plans add more design and business tools.

What is Showtime?

Showtime is LIW Cards’ audience-first card experience for artists, performers and creators, including musicians, DJs, producers, comedians, podcasters, actors and creators/influencers. It is available on Free, Lite, Plus and Pro.

Is Showtime only for music artists?

No. Music is one set of Showtime tools. A comedian can focus on Shows, Videos, Gallery and Book Me; a podcaster can focus on Podcast, Media, Social and Book Me.

Do people need an app to view my card?

No. Your LIW Card opens as a web page on a compatible phone browser, so the person receiving your card does not need to install an LIW Cards app.

Can I share my card with a QR code?

Yes. QR sharing lets someone scan your code with their phone and open your digital card. You can use the QR code on screen or place it on supported printed materials.

Can I update my card after I share it?

Yes. Because the information lives online, you can update your card without replacing every link or QR code you already shared.

Can a digital business card show services and products?

Eligible LIW Cards plans can include richer business content such as services, products, lead tools, video and other sections. Check the pricing page for current plan availability.
