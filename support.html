Quick answers

Common support questions

These steps solve the most frequent problems before a message is needed.

My changes are not saving. What should I check?

Keep the editor open until it shows “Saved to server.” Confirm the device is online, then refresh once. When the message does not appear, include the card name, the field you changed, and a screenshot in your support request.

Why does the editor preview look different from the public card?

First save the card, then open the external preview again. A hard refresh can clear an older cached design. Include both screenshots when the difference remains.

How does the Plus or Pro 7-day trial work?

Eligible first-time customers pay $0 for seven days and provide a payment method at checkout. The selected annual plan starts billing after the trial unless it is canceled before the trial ends.

How do I cancel or update billing?

Open the Dashboard and choose Manage billing. Stripe’s secure customer portal handles payment-method updates, invoices, cancellations, and renewal details.

What should an affiliate include in a support request?

Include the account email, referral code, the affected customer when applicable, and a screenshot of the page or error.
